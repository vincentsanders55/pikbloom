Common questions

Before you ask.

What file formats do you need from me?
Anything works. JPEG, PNG, even a link to your website. We'll extract what we need.
What product categories do you work with?
Shoes, jewelry, perfume, clothing, accessories, and more. If it's a physical product, we can shoot it.
Do I keep the rights to the images?
Yes. Full ownership transfers to you upon delivery. Use them anywhere — no restrictions.
Can I test the studio before ordering?
Absolutely. Tell us about your products and we'll create sample visuals at no cost. No commitment.
Will the result look beautiful and natural?
That's our entire promise. We obsess over getting the right lighting, the natural drape, the accurate colors. Our visuals sit alongside traditional photography on live e-commerce sites — and customers can't tell the difference. The free sample lets you see it on your own products.
What if something doesn't feel right?
We refine until you love it. Background, framing, styling, mood — we adjust whatever needs adjusting. Getting the result right matters more to us than shipping fast.
How does AI fit into your process?
AI is our creative engine, but the eye is human. Every visual is reviewed for realism, coherence, and beauty before it reaches you. The technology lets us go faster — not cut corners.
